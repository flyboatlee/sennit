隐私政策

最后更新 2026-10-28 · 生效日期 2026-10-28

一句话：Sennit 没有服务器、没有账号体系、不做任何统计。 你的资产、凭据、会话录制与文档只存在你自己的 Mac 上，我们看不到。

一、我们不收集什么

下面这些是同类软件通常会做、而这个应用不做的：

  • 不收集设备标识符、IP 地址、使用时长或功能点击等使用数据；
  • 不接入任何第三方分析、广告或崩溃上报 SDK；
  • 不要求注册或登录，没有云端同步，没有遥测开关（因为不存在遥测）；
  • 当前版本不检查更新，也不会定期向任何地址发请求。

二、你的数据存在哪里

全部在本机。~/Library/Application Support/Sennit/ 下：

内容 位置 保护方式
资产、分组、凭据、片段、端口转发规则、设置 store/store.db.enc 整库 AES-256-GCM 加密，密钥由你的主密码派生
会话录制 recordings/ asciicast v2 明文文件，仅本机；需要保密请自行加密磁盘
运行日志 logs/ 仅记录连接状态与错误信息，不记录密码或私钥内容
笔记、备份、导入的配置 你在系统对话框里选择的位置 备份可选口令密封（AES-256-GCM + PBKDF2 派生密钥）

主密码不会写入磁盘。你可以选择把解锁凭据交给 macOS 系统钥匙串托管，此时由系统保护， Sennit 自身不保存主密码。界面不会回显已保存的口令。

三、应用会发起的网络连接

只有下面三类，全部由你的操作触发：

  1. 你配置的远程主机 —— SSH、Telnet、SFTP、端口转发（本地 / 动态 / 远程）。 连接从你的 Mac 直连目标机器，我们不参与、不中转。
  2. 设备 Web 管理页 —— 仅官网直发版提供内嵌浏览器，打开的是你在资产里填写的地址。 Mac App Store 版不含此功能，也不会加载任何网页。
  3. AI 助手 —— 默认关闭，需要你自己填写接口地址与密钥，详见下一节。

除此之外，应用不会主动发起其他出站请求：不拉取远程配置、不上报使用情况、不查询更新。

四、AI 助手会把什么发出去

只有在你配置了 AI 服务并主动提问时才会发生：

  • 发什么：你的提问、当前对话上下文，以及你主动附加的材料 （终端屏幕上的文本、命令回执、打开文档的片段）。
  • 发到哪：你自己填写的那个接口地址（OpenAI 兼容的 /chat/completions）。我们不是该地址的运营方，既不中转也不保留副本。
  • 对方怎么处理：取决于该服务商自己的隐私政策 —— 请只把你信任的服务配进来。
  • 不配置 AI 服务时，应用不会产生任何 AI 请求。

五、剪贴板

只有你点击「复制」时才会写入系统剪贴板：复制命令、复制设备登录用户名或口令、复制远程桌面口令。 复制口令后可以开启自动清空。应用不会主动读取剪贴板内容。

六、文件访问

SFTP 本地目录、笔记库、备份与导出，只访问你在系统文件对话框里选择的位置。 Mac App Store 版受 App Sandbox 限制，只能访问你明确授权过的目录。应用不扫描磁盘。

七、第三方共享

我们不出售、不出租、不共享你的数据 —— 我们手上没有你的数据。 唯一的第三方是你自己配置的 AI 服务端（见第四节）。

八、数据保留与删除

我们不持有数据，因此没有「保留期」。数据留在你选择的位置，直到你删除它：

  • 删除保险库文件，或卸载应用 —— 注意 macOS 卸载应用不会自动删除 ~/Library/Application Support/Sennit/，需要手动删除该目录；
  • 录制、笔记、备份是独立文件，可直接删除。

删除不可恢复。主密码丢失后我们无法解密你的库 —— 没有后门，也没有托管密钥。

九、儿童

本应用是面向专业运维人员的工作工具，不面向 13 岁以下儿童，也不会收集儿童信息。

十、政策变更

政策变更会在本页更新并注明日期；涉及数据处理的实质变更会在应用内提示。

十一、联系我们

任何隐私相关问题：lifb_tc@126.com

Privacy Policy

Last updated 2026-10-28 · Effective 2026-10-28

In one sentence: Sennit has no servers, no accounts and no analytics. Your hosts, credentials, session recordings and documents stay on your own Mac — we never see them.

1. What we do not collect

These are things comparable tools commonly do, and this app does not:

  • No device identifiers, IP addresses, usage duration or feature-click data;
  • No third-party analytics, advertising or crash-reporting SDK;
  • No sign-up or sign-in, no cloud sync, and no telemetry toggle (there is no telemetry);
  • This version does not check for updates and does not contact any address periodically.

2. Where your data lives

Entirely on your Mac, under ~/Library/Application Support/Sennit/:

Content Location Protection
Hosts, groups, credentials, snippets, port-forwarding rules, settings store/store.db.enc Whole database encrypted with AES-256-GCM; key derived from your master password
Session recordings recordings/ asciicast v2 files in plain text, local only; encrypt your disk if they must be confidential
Runtime logs logs/ Connection status and errors only — never passwords or private key material
Notes, backups, imported configuration Wherever you pick in the system dialog Backups may be sealed with a passphrase (AES-256-GCM with a PBKDF2-derived key)

The master password is never written to disk. You may hand the unlock secret to the macOS Keychain, in which case the system protects it and Sennit stores no master password. Saved passwords are never echoed back into the interface.

3. Network connections the app makes

Only the following three kinds, all triggered by you:

  1. The remote hosts you configure — SSH, Telnet, SFTP and port forwarding (local / dynamic / remote). Connections go from your Mac straight to the target; we are not in the path.
  2. Device web consoles — only the direct-download build ships an embedded browser, and it opens the address stored in your host entry. The Mac App Store build does not include this feature and loads no web pages at all.
  3. The AI assistant — off by default; requires an endpoint and key that you supply. See the next section.

Beyond these, the app makes no outbound requests: no remote configuration, no usage reporting, no update checks.

4. What the AI assistant sends

This only happens when you have configured an AI service and ask a question:

  • What is sent: your question, the current conversation context, and any material you explicitly attach (text from the terminal screen, command receipts, excerpts of an open document).
  • Where it goes: the endpoint you entered yourself (an OpenAI-compatible /chat/completions URL). We do not operate that endpoint, proxy it, or keep a copy.
  • What happens next depends on that provider's own privacy policy — only configure a service you trust.
  • Without a configured AI service, no AI request is ever made.

5. Clipboard

The app writes to the system clipboard only when you click a copy action: copy a command, copy a device login name or password, copy a remote-desktop password. Password copies can auto-clear after a configurable delay. The app does not read clipboard contents on its own.

6. File access

The SFTP local pane, note library, backups and exports only touch locations you choose in a system file dialog. The Mac App Store build runs in App Sandbox and can only reach folders you have explicitly granted. The app never scans your disk.

7. Sharing with third parties

We do not sell, rent or share your data — we do not hold any. The only third party involved is the AI endpoint you configure yourself (section 4).

8. Retention and deletion

We hold no data, so there is no retention period on our side. Your data stays where you put it until you delete it:

  • Delete the vault file, or uninstall the app — note that uninstalling on macOS does not remove ~/Library/Application Support/Sennit/; delete that folder yourself;
  • Recordings, notes and backups are ordinary files you can delete directly.

Deletion is irreversible. If the master password is lost, we cannot decrypt your vault — there is no backdoor and no escrowed key.

9. Children

This app is a work tool for professional system administrators. It is not directed to children under 13 and does not collect information from them.

10. Changes

Changes to this policy will be published on this page with an updated date. Material changes to data handling will also be surfaced inside the app.

11. Contact

Privacy questions: lifb_tc@126.com
